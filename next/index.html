flint-ntcharts — terminal styling (next)

Every case compiled by the current terminal-style branches of flint-ntcharts and ntcharts, at 72×18 and at 44×15, drawn cell by cell as a terminal draws them.
  • Axis labels in the fewest characters that still say where you are: 2026 Feb Mar, 2020 Jul 2021 Jul, 1880s 1900s; names that cannot fit get an ellipsis, or the bars turn horizontal.
  • Round values on every measured axis (nice ticks, right axis included), as Flint's own scales do.
  • Value labels on bars where they fit; symbols that set scatter series apart; a zero line where Y crosses zero.
  • A frame: title, legend and axis titles, given up in turn when the plot needs the rows. Flint theme inks.
